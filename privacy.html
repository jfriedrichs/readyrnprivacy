Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

In short: ReadyRN does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers. Your study progress stays on your own device.

This policy explains how the ReadyRN NCLEX-RN study app ("ReadyRN", "the app") handles information. It applies to the ReadyRN iOS app and the ReadyRN web app.

Information we collect

We don't collect any information about you. ReadyRN does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it does not need an account.

Information stored on your device

To save your study progress, ReadyRN keeps the following on your device only:

  • Which practice questions you have answered and whether you answered them correctly
  • Questions you have flagged for review
  • Your practice and simulation results
  • An unfinished study session, so you can resume it

This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Reset all progress at the bottom of the app's Dashboard, or by deleting the app (or, for the web app, clearing your browser's website data).

Analytics, advertising and tracking

ReadyRN has no analytics, advertising or tracking code. It doesn't use cookies to track you, doesn't track you across other apps or websites, and doesn't include third-party software that collects data.

Network use

The iOS app includes all of its content and works completely offline. The web app downloads its own files from the website that hosts it so it can work offline afterward. Like any website, that hosting service may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy policy. ReadyRN does not receive or use those logs.

Apple services

ReadyRN is distributed through Apple's App Store and TestFlight. If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple may share limited information with the developer, such as your name and email if you were invited by email, the device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback you choose to send. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy. We use beta information only to improve the app.

Children's privacy

ReadyRN is intended for nursing students and is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Sharing and selling of data

We don't sell, rent or share personal information, because we don't collect any.

Medical disclaimer

ReadyRN is an educational study aid. Its content is not medical advice and is not a substitute for professional judgment, official NCSBN materials or your nursing program's instruction.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If the app ever begins collecting information, this policy will be updated before that happens.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@motorcoachnav.com.
